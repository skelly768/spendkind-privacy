Privacy Policy for SpendKind

Effective date: 17 January 2026

SpendKind is designed to help users manage their personal spending in a private and respectful way. Your privacy is important to us.

Information Collection and Use

SpendKind does not require account creation and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or payment details.

All spending data entered into the app is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Advertising

SpendKind may offer optional advertisements to support the app. Advertisements are provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob), which may collect anonymous usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

SpendKind does not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

Children’s Privacy

SpendKind does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
